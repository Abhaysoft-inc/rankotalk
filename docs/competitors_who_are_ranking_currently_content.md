JEE Main College Predictor 2026: JoSAA Counselling
Shikha’s JEE Main 2026 College Predictor is linked to JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) counselling process. The JoSAA counselling is a centralised system for admission into NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. Qualified candidates must register on the JoSAA portal, fill their choices of colleges and branches, and participate in multiple rounds of seat allotment for IIT JEE admission.

JoSAA conducts several rounds where seats are allotted based on All India Rank, category (Gen/OBC/SC/ST/EWS), gender, and Home State/Other State quota. Cutoffs change every round depending on seat availability and student preferences. This is where the JEE Main college predictor 2026 becomes useful; it uses previous years’ JoSAA opening and closing ranks to estimate realistic options before choice filling. A well-informed strategy during counselling will improve the chances of securing a preferred branch and institute.

Understanding JOSAA Counselling
Institution Types
IITs (Indian Institutes of Technology): Premier institutions for engineering, admission is exclusively through the JEE Advanced exam.
NITs (National Institutes of Technology): Top-tier technical institutes with 50% of seats reserved under the Home State (HS) quota. Admission is through the JEE Main exam.
IIITs (Indian Institutes of Information Technology): These institutes focus on Information Technology and related fields. Most admit students through JEE Main under an All India (AI) quota.
GFTIs (Government Funded Technical Institutes): A diverse group of institutes funded by the government, admitting students through JEE Main.
Admission Quotas Explained
AI (All India): This quota is open to students from any state in India. It is the primary quota for all IITs and most IIITs.
HS (Home State): Reserved for candidates who have completed their qualifying exam (Class 12th) from the same state where the institute (mainly NITs) is located. This significantly increases admission chances for local students.
OS (Other State): This applies to candidates applying to an institute (mainly NITs) located outside their home state.
Special Quotas (GO, JK, LA): These are specific sub-quotas within the Home State quota for candidates from Goa, Jammu & Kashmir, and Ladakh, applicable at their respective NITs.
