After you are allotted a seat in JoSAA, you must upload documents and, in some cases, report to the institute. A missing or wrongly formatted document is a common reason for a seat being cancelled. This checklist helps you get ready before the first round, not after it.

## Before you begin

The official list changes slightly from year to year, so treat this as a checklist to prepare early. The authoritative list is in the JoSAA information bulletin and on the allotment page. Scan every document clearly, in the file size and format the portal asks for.

## Core documents for everyone

- **JEE Main admit card and score card or rank letter.**
- **Class 10 certificate or marksheet**, which is used as proof of date of birth.
- **Class 12 marksheet or passing certificate**, which also helps confirm your state of eligibility.
- **Photo identity proof**, such as Aadhaar or another government ID.
- **Passport-size photographs** as specified in the notice.
- **JoSAA seat allotment letter** and the fee payment receipt, which are generated on the portal.

## Category documents

If you are claiming a reservation, the certificate is as important as your rank.

- **OBC-NCL:** a valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate in the central government format. Check the date requirements in the bulletin, because the certificate usually has to be valid for the relevant year.
- **EWS:** a valid Economically Weaker Section certificate in the central government format for the relevant financial year.
- **SC or ST:** a caste certificate issued by a competent authority.
- **PwD:** a disability certificate issued by a competent medical authority, with the percentage of disability.

State-format certificates are often not accepted in place of the central format, so check this first. Getting a certificate corrected can take days or weeks, so apply early.

## Documents for specific situations

- **Home State quota:** proof of the state where you passed Class 12. Your Class 12 certificate usually covers this, and the bulletin lists other accepted proofs.
- **Gap year or improvement attempts:** your Class 12 passing year must meet the eligibility rules in the bulletin.
- **Name differences:** if your name differs between documents, keep a gazette notification or affidavit ready.
- **Migration, transfer or character certificate:** institutes often ask for these at physical reporting. Check the institute’s own notice.
- **Medical fitness certificate:** some institutes ask for one in their own format.

## Tips for uploading

1. Scan documents in colour at a readable resolution, and check that the name and numbers are clear.
2. Keep file names simple, and keep a folder with every file in the format required.
3. Use the same name spelling that appears in your JEE Main application.
4. Upload before the deadline, not on the last day, in case the portal is slow.
5. Keep several printed copies of your documents for physical reporting, if the institute asks for them.

## Common mistakes

- Using a certificate in the wrong format, or one that has expired for the year.
- Uploading a blurred or cropped scan.
- Not having a category certificate at all and finding out after the allotment.
- Missing the last date for fee payment or document upload.
- Forgetting the institute’s own separate reporting instructions.

## After you upload

The institute reviews your documents and either accepts them or gives you a chance to correct them. Watch your email and the portal during this period. If your documents are rejected and you do not correct them in time, the seat can be cancelled.
